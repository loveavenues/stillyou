13 Months of Us 💫

REFLECTING ON US

A year and one month. Thinking of all that we have been through, shared, and survived as a couple, that feels like an entire lifetime through many nights spent awake, talks, laughs, misunderstandings, reassurance, patience, and love. Thank you for being there. Thank you for choosing us even on the days when it's tough to figure out what I am feeling, when life is overwhelming, and when it feels like the future is uncertain.

I know hindi naging madali lahat. We've learned how to navigate through tough talks, adjustments, waiting, overthinking, letting emotions get the best of us, and the constant reminder of why we started. We manage to find our way back to each other after every argument.

You became my peace after a day filled with stress, and the person I always want to talk to first. Everything feels better because it involves you.

I love how caring you are. I love how hard you try. I love your gentleness, your strength, your attitude, your smile, your voice, your kiss, your everything (lalo na smell hehe). And even after 13 months, I can still find myself staring at you like I've just fallen in love with you for the very first time.

You have become one of the best things to happen to me. I hope we keep growing together. More months. More memories. More random calls. More kisses. More "I miss you" & "I love you" messages. More laughter. More pikunan. More love.

But there is one thing that I want you to take away from this letter, and that is that no matter what else may come, I will never stop being thankful for having loved someone as wonderful as you. No words can express how much I love you, Adi.

Happy 13th month, baby.
